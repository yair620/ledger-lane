Field notes / Payment Clarity

About / editorial posture

A useful site should make uncertainty visible.

Payment Clarity is an independent information project about crypto payment choices, verification records, and promotion terms.

Our editorial approach

We write from first principles: identify the decision, list the conditions that could change it, and separate a documented fact from an assumption. We prefer a short checklist over a confident prediction.

Our content is informational and general. It is not financial, legal, tax, or gambling advice. Operators, rules, and payment methods can change; always use the current source and local requirements.

Independence and commercial links

Some links are affiliate links and may generate a commission for us. We label them before the click, keep the disclosure adjacent to the link, and do not control the destination’s terms. A commission never buys a guarantee or overrides a reader’s own decision.

Corrections

Because terms and infrastructure change, date-sensitive pages should be rechecked. If you spot a factual or accessibility problem, preserve the page URL and contact details so the correction can be traced.

Our promise

Useful checklists. Visible uncertainty. No invented facts.
