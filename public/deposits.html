Deposits / before money moves

Make the first transaction boring.

The safest deposit is the one you understand before sending. Compare the method, network, minimum, fee, and confirmation policy rather than chasing a headline number.

A pre-deposit checklist

  1. Confirm eligibility. Check age, location, account status, and the operator’s supported countries.
  2. Read the limits. Record minimum and maximum deposit values, daily caps, and any method-specific exclusions.
  3. Match networks. A wallet address for one chain is not interchangeable with another. Verify the symbol and network in the destination platform.
  4. Estimate the total cost. Include network fee, platform fee, spread, and any conversion charged by your wallet or exchange.
  5. Start small. If the terms permit it, use a small test and wait for a complete confirmation before deciding whether to continue.

What a transaction record should contain

Keep the transaction hash, network, amount sent, amount received, fee, timestamp, and the page that displayed the instructions. A support ticket with these details is more useful than a vague description of “the deposit did not work.”

Slow down. Do not send funds to an address copied from an unsolicited message. Re-open the official service and verify the destination independently.

When the screen says “processing”

Blockchain confirmations, internal review, and account verification are different clocks. Check the operator’s published processing window, then wait until that window has passed before opening repeated tickets. Never pay a third party to “unlock” a withdrawal.
