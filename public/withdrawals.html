Withdrawals / proof before pressure

A withdrawal should be explained, not improvised.

Use this page to prepare before requesting funds. Requirements change, so the operator’s current terms and support response are the source of truth.

Before you request a payout

  • Confirm the destination is yours and supported by the service.
  • Check minimum withdrawal, network fee, processing time, and any verification requirement.
  • Make sure your account email, identity details, and source-of-funds information are current.
  • Use a small test withdrawal first when possible, then wait for the full round trip before scaling up.
  • Record the request ID, date, destination, amount, and every support response.

If a payout is delayed

First check the published queue and whether the delay is caused by network confirmation, an internal review, or missing documents. Send one concise support request with transaction IDs and timestamps. Avoid sending repeated “please hurry” messages from multiple accounts, and never pay someone who offers to release funds.

Slow down. A support agent who asks for an extra payment, remote access, or secret code is a red flag. Stop and verify through the official channel.

Small habits that reduce uncertainty

Save a copy of every terms page you relied on. Confirm the address on both the service and your wallet. Check the final received amount, not only the amount sent. A clean trail takes less time than reconstructing one under stress.
