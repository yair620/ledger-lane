The short version

Clarity is a safety feature.

Promotions can be time-bound, region-specific, and easy to misread. A two-minute check now can prevent a costly misunderstanding later.

01 / BEFORE

Map the offer

Capture the exact bonus language, expiry, wagering requirement, eligible currency, and country restriction before you deposit.

Verify the exit
